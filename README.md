## 9. The Educated Guess

### Simple model
If you treat each map as an independent coin flip where Vitality wins a map with probability *p*:

| Vitality map win % (p) | VIT 3–0 | VIT 3–1 | VIT 3–2 | **VIT series** | MOUZ 3–2 | MOUZ 3–1 | MOUZ 3–0 | **MOUZ series** |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 52% | 14.1% | 20.2% | 19.4% | **53.7%** | 17.9% | 17.3% | 11.1% | **46.3%** |
| 54% | 15.7% | 21.7% | 20.0% | **57.5%** | 17.0% | 15.8% | 9.7% | **42.5%** |
| **56% (my estimate)** | 17.6% | **23.2%** | 20.4% | **61.1%** | 16.0% | 14.3% | 8.5% | **38.9%** |
| 58% | 19.5% | 24.6% | 20.7% | **64.7%** | 15.0% | 12.9% | 7.4% | **35.3%** |
| 60% (market-implied) | 21.6% | 25.9% | 20.7% | **68.3%** | 13.8% | 11.5% | 6.4% | **31.7%** |

The market's ~67% implies Vitality wins ~59–60% of individual maps. **I land at ~56% per map → ~61% for the series**, slightly below the market. The reasons are MOUZ's form on Mirage, Inferno and Nuke, their 10–1 map run this week, and the fact that this is the first meeting of the full lineups.

### Final prediction: **Vitality 3–1** (≈60% Vitality / ≈40% MOUZ)

**Most likely story:** Vitality takes their Cache/Dust2 pick. MOUZ takes their pick (Mirage or Nuke). The swing maps (Inferno, and whichever of Dust2/Mirage isn't picked) go to Vitality, mostly through ZywOo/ropz firepower and their composure in close rounds.

**What makes MOUZ win (upset path, ~40%):**
1. MOUZ win their own picks **and** steal Dust2 again (they've won the last two Dust2s in the H2H).
2. Spinx and torzsi stay at 1.25+ and PR keeps up his QF/SF aggression.
3. apEX and mezii repeat their semi-final (sub-0.75 ratings), leaving ZywOo and ropz to carry four players' worth of impact.
4. MOUZ gets the decider on a map where it's favored on form (Mirage/Nuke).

**What makes Vitality win comfortably (3–0, ~18%):** Vitality stomps Cache. ZywOo's form from the SF continues. MOUZ's history of folding in finals against Vitality (0–3 since 2025) repeats.
